01 / Project overview

Editorial desire.
Decision confidence.

LuxRoom is a portfolio-grade furniture commerce prototype for a high-consideration purchase: inspiration early, then progressively stronger evidence about size, finish, material, delivery and room fit.

Project modePortfolio
prototype

Public static HTML/CSS/JS experience.

Primary usersDesign-conscious
shoppers

People evaluating furniture for real rooms.

Core taskDiscover →
evaluate

Move from room inspiration into a confident decision.

PlatformResponsive
web

Desktop-first with tablet/mobile adaptation.

Problem

Premium imagery creates desire, but appearance alone cannot answer fit, access, finish, timing and delivery questions.

Those questions become part of the product experience rather than checkout-only detail.

Goal

Connect inspiration to useful evidence without flattening the brand into a dense marketplace catalogue.

Working direction: Quiet Architectural Commerce.

Role

Product/UX/UI direction, visual system, responsive interaction, coded prototype and QA evidence.

Core surfaces

Home, Collection, Rooms, PDP, Compare, Saved Room, Wishlist, Cart, Checkout, Tracking, Account, Story and Contact.

Constraints

Compact static catalogue; no live inventory/payment backend; brand direction is proposed from project assets rather than an official brand book.

Product truth

Checkout and contact are simulated; no real-payment, CRM, inventory or architectural-fit guarantee is claimed.

LuxRoomStatic portfolio evidence screen for editable Figma import. No JavaScript, lazy loading or runtime-generated layers.
