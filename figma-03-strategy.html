03 / UX & product strategy

Confidence grows
as commitment grows.

Information density changes across the journey: inspiration first, then progressively more technical evidence as the shopper approaches a decision.

Problem statement

How might LuxRoom preserve a premium editorial experience while making expensive furniture easier to evaluate for a real space?

Primary behavior

Room inspiration → category → product → finish → dimensions → compare/save → delivery → checkout or consultation.

01

Context before conversion

Understand the object and room before purchase pressure.

02

Evidence near commitment

Dimensions, finish, arrival and fit move closer to primary actions.

03

Progressive disclosure

Technical depth without turning discovery into a spreadsheet.

04

Truth over theatre

No fake scarcity, fabricated reviews or pseudo-AR certainty.

Success signal

Users can explain dimensions, finish, delivery expectation and fit uncertainty before commitment. This is a design target, not a measured production uplift.

Recovery

When fit or product information remains uncertain, the next step is explicit: adjust, compare, save or request guidance.
