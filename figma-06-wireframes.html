06 / Wireframes & exploration

Explore the structure
before polishing it.

The exploration focuses on composition and hierarchy. The selected direction combines editorial storytelling with decision evidence instead of choosing either a pure catalogue or a pure magazine surface.

PDP direction

Three ways to organize commitment.

A / CatalogueRejected

Efficient but visually generic; brand story and technical evidence compete.

B / EditorialRejected

Strong atmosphere but delays dimensions, finish and delivery too far down the journey.

C / HybridSelected

Media + decision panel first, followed by progressive technical and editorial depth.
