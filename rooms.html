06 / How we read a room

Three things before
the shopping list.

01

Proportion

Check the room, circulation and the largest object first. The right piece still has to leave space around it.

02

Light

Notice where daylight arrives and where the room needs a softer layer after dark. Lighting changes how every material reads.

03

Material

Repeat fewer materials with intention. Texture and finish can connect separate objects without making the room feel matched.
