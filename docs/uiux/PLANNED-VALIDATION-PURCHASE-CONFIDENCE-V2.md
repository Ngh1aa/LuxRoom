# LuxRoom — Purchase Confidence V2 Planned Validation

**Status:** PLANNED VALIDATION — no participant findings or conversion impact are claimed.

## Product question

How might LuxRoom help a high-consideration furniture buyer decide whether a piece fits the room, can physically enter the space, suits the selected finish/material expectations, and can arrive on an acceptable timeline before committing to checkout or consultation?

## Prototype scope under test

- Product detail fit guidance
- Compare decision matrix
- Saved Room decision summary
- Per-piece room fit / lead-time / returnability evidence
- Consultation handoff with room constraints and saved pieces carried forward
- Checkout delivery/access details

## Participant target

5 participants who have bought, considered buying, or helped choose medium/high-consideration furniture for a home within the last 24 months.

This is a directional usability sample, not statistically representative research.

## Core scenario

> You are furnishing a living room. Choose a sofa or chair, decide whether it is likely to fit the room and doorway, compare it with another option, save the preferred pieces, then decide whether you are confident enough to continue or want a consultation.

Use the same room constraints for all participants:

- room width: 420 cm
- room depth: 360 cm
- narrowest doorway: 88 cm
- desired circulation clearance: 60 cm

## Tasks

1. Find a piece appropriate for the room.
2. Identify overall dimensions and selected finish.
3. Use **Will it fit?** and explain the result in your own words.
4. Compare two pieces and state the trade-off that matters most.
5. Save the preferred option(s) to Saved Room.
6. Interpret the Saved Room summary: fit/access, saved value and longest lead time.
7. Start a room consultation and verify whether the room context carried forward correctly.
8. Explain what information is still missing before a real purchase.

## Measures

### Primary
- Task completion without moderator rescue.
- Correct interpretation of fit guidance.
- Correct interpretation that fit guidance is prototype guidance, not a delivery guarantee.
- Successful consultation handoff without re-entering room measurements.

### Secondary
- Time to locate dimensions / fit / delivery information.
- Number of navigation reversals.
- Number of incorrect assumptions about stock, returns or delivery.
- Self-reported decision confidence (1–5) before and after using the decision tools.

### Guardrails
- Do not optimize for fewer clicks if users miss delivery/access constraints.
- Do not present “Likely fits” as guaranteed fit.
- Do not imply real stock, payment, CRM submission or fulfilment.
- Consultation must remain secondary to purchase, not a competing primary CTA.

## Evidence capture

For every session record:

- task outcome;
- notable hesitation;
- misunderstood label;
- missing information;
- exact screen where uncertainty occurs;
- design change proposed from that observation.

No fabricated quotes. No invented success percentages.

## Iteration rule

Only change UI after a repeated usability problem, a severe single-session failure, or a clear accessibility/runtime defect. Preserve the current LuxRoom visual direction unless evidence shows that the visual treatment itself causes the problem.

## Portfolio evidence format

Case-study update should show:

**Uncertainty → current interface → observed failure → design decision → revised interface → retest result**

If testing has not happened yet, label the section **Planned validation** rather than **Outcome**.
