12 / Iteration & handoff

Inspect the pixels.
Repair the cause.

This final screen uses actual repository QA history rather than fabricated usability metrics. It shows a material responsive defect, the owning layer, the repair and the rules that should survive handoff.

Before / QA-P1-01

Gallery consumed the first viewport.

At 390 × 844, gallery, thumbnails and fit cue pushed the product name below the initial view. Price and sticky Add to cart could appear before clear product identity.

→
After / repaired owner

Media depth reduced, identity restored.

Mobile gallery and evidence spacing were tightened in the owning PDP CSS. Title, price and description now enter the first viewport while scale/material imagery remains available.

Tokens

Semantic foundations

Reuse paper/canvas/ink/clay/stone, 4px spacing, focus/success/error roles and shared type variables.

Responsive

Recompose, do not scale

Mobile navigation removes hover dependency; filters and PDP evidence change structure.

States

Never color-only

Selected, error, disabled and success combine semantic color with copy or boundaries.

Motion

Restrained and optional

Shared durations/easing; essential information never depends on animation; reduced motion supported.

System truth

Prototype boundaries

Checkout/payment and contact delivery remain simulated. No live inventory, CRM or fit guarantee.

Accessibility

Baseline, not certification

Focus-visible, native semantics and reduced motion are preserved; no formal WCAG conformance claim.

Rendered scope

Home, Collection, PDP, Story, Contact, Cart and Checkout at representative desktop/mobile sizes plus tablet/laptop pressure points.

System reality

Search, filtering, cart and wishlist are real within the static browser implementation; simulated surfaces stay labelled truthfully.

Unknowns

No production analytics or LuxRoom-specific moderated usability evidence is claimed. Those remain future validation inputs.
