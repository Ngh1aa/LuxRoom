07 / Design system

Quiet by design.
Useful by default.

A restrained system for premium furniture commerce: editorial typography, soft mineral neutrals, sharp geometry and deliberate interaction states.

Built from the same tokens used across the live LuxRoom prototype.

Color system

Warm neutrals,
one grounded accent.

Color is used for hierarchy and confidence rather than decoration. Clay carries brand warmth; ink carries action; mineral surfaces create calm depth.

Typography

Editorial presence.
Digital restraint.

Newsreader gives product storytelling a soft editorial voice. DM Sans handles navigation, data, filters and practical decision-making.

Spacing & layout

A 4px rhythm
with generous pauses.

Dense utility moments sit inside broad editorial whitespace. Content is capped at 1360px with responsive gutters and a 4px spacing scale.
